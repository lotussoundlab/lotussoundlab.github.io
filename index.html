PETER KOLESNIKOV
Sound Engineer · Founder of Lotus Sound Lab

I've worked independently across music, audio production and post-production since 2019, based in Tel Aviv. My work spans recording vocals and instruments, session preparation, mixing, mastering and vocal production, alongside dialogue and podcast editing, audio restoration, noise reduction, sound design and music production. I hold a five-year Specialist Degree in Musical Sound Engineering from the Institute of Contemporary Art (ISI), Moscow, completed in 2023.
